# Biden Unleashed

Recovered source from a 2020 generative-media / Twitch experiment.

I’m a progressive, and this was never meant as some anti-Biden political project. The premise was much simpler: GPT-2 was an incoherent mess, and to me Biden’s public speech already sounded like brain soup. Training one incoherent thing on the other was the joke. I wanted to take that past a text-generation demo and turn it into a character people could actually interact with on Twitch.

So I collected public Biden speeches and interviews, built a text corpus, fine-tuned GPT-2, worked with segmented speech audio and voice synthesis, and connected the output to a 3D character / livestream pipeline.

The generated text is synthetic. It should not be represented as an authentic Biden quote or statement.

## Why the character looked like that

The exaggerated, busty body was deliberate bait.

At the time I was designing for what I understood as a heavily male Twitch audience, and I wanted the character to grab attention before anyone even knew what they were looking at. Then the rest of the joke could land: an absurd synthetic Biden avatar talking nonsense generated by a model trained on his own lexicon.

The body was there to exploit the male gaze and push the absurdity further, not to mock women, trans people, or any other marginalized group. That was never the target of the joke.

## The live system

The project grew well past a simple text-generation demo.

One machine handled Twitch input, generated responses, produced audio, and passed files across my home network. A second machine handled playback / rendering and the actual Twitch broadcast through OBS.

I flashed my router with DD-WRT because I wanted more control over the network and a better way to reach the setup remotely. That helped with access and recovery, but it obviously could not make an unreliable connection reliable. I also used remote access, restart automation, and a network-connected power strip so I could bring machines back when something froze.

The full runtime glue has not all been recovered, but the surviving source and notes document the model training, generation, transcript/audio preparation, iClone lip-sync work, and parts of the later live-media pipeline.

## It actually got Twitch Partner

The channel eventually reached Twitch Partner status, which was pretty wild for what started as a stupid GPT-2 experiment.

Keeping it alive was the harder part. This was peak COVID, before vaccines. I was avoiding the subway and splitting time between Queens and my then-partner’s place, sometimes literally walking about seven miles each way. Meanwhile the stream depended on two computers, a home network, remote power / restart workarounds, and machines that could freeze or run out of memory. A bad connection or crash could mean days of downtime.

DD-WRT gave me more control, but it could not remove the basic problem: there were too many failure points in an always-on homemade stream, and physically babysitting them did not fit the logistics of my life at the time.

At a certain point, keeping the character running 24/7 just was not realistic, so I stopped maintaining the live stream.

## What survived

- `src/format_transcripts.py` — converts per-source transcript text into pipe-delimited filename/text metadata suitable for pairing transcript lines with numbered audio clips.
- `src/train_biden.py` — fine-tunes the 355M GPT-2 model with `gpt-2-simple` using the recovered training corpus workflow.
- `src/generate_biden.py` — loads a fine-tuned checkpoint and generates synthetic text from a caller-supplied prefix.
- `src/iclone/load_audio_viseme.py` — cleaned iClone Python proof-of-concept for loading audio onto an avatar and invoking lip-sync / viseme handling.
- `archive/aitextgen/rev_transcript_scraper.py` — historical Selenium-based transcript collection / cleanup script.
- `archive/aitextgen/custom_tokenizer_train.py` — recovered custom-tokenizer training experiment.
- `DATASET_NOTES.md` — inventory of recovered audio/transcript material without republishing the media or full transcript corpus.
- `RUNTIME_NOTES.md` — reconstructed notes for the later two-computer / Twitch runtime.
- `ASSET_NOTES.md` — inventory of large Character Creator / iClone assets intentionally left out of GitHub.

## Historical environment

The original project used `gpt-2-simple` 0.7.1 and its TensorFlow 1.x-era stack. Modern Python/TensorFlow installations are unlikely to run this unchanged.

The original training script selected the GPT-2 `355M` model and requested up to 100,000 fine-tuning steps. Those values are preserved as defaults in the recovered script.

## Archive cleanup

The recovered folders also contained hundreds of WAV clips, full speech/interview corpora, duplicate media, model checkpoints, and vendored third-party files. Those are intentionally not committed here.

This repository preserves the project-specific code without republishing large source media or pretending every piece of the original live system has been recovered.
